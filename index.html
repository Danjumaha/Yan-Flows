Frequently Asked Questions

How does the coin system work?

Each successful order deducts 1 coin from your balance. You receive 10 free coins when you start. When coins run low, purchase more via Paystack. No coins = no new orders processed, but your store stays live.

Do I need a website or domain?

No! YAN-FLOWS provides your storefront at yan-flows.com/store/yourusername. It's mobile-optimized, fast, and ready to share on WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, or anywhere.

How do WhatsApp orders work?

When a customer checks out, YAN-FLOWS generates a prefilled WhatsApp message with order details, delivery info, and payment instructions. The customer clicks "Send" and the order lands in your WhatsApp instantly.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. We use Paystack (PCI-DSS compliant) for all transactions. We never store your bank details. Firebase security rules protect all user data with enterprise-grade encryption.

Can I use YAN-FLOWS on my phone?

Yes! YAN-FLOWS is fully responsive. Manage your store, products, and orders from any device — iPhone, Android, tablet, or desktop. No app download required.
