Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about YAN-FLOWS.

How do I create my storefront?
Sign up as a seller, complete your business profile in Seller Setup, and add products. Your store will be live at yan-flows.com/store/yourname.
How does the coin system work?
Each successful order deducts 1 coin. You get 10 free coins on signup. Purchase more via Paystack when you run low.
Can I use YAN-FLOWS on mobile?
Yes! YAN-FLOWS is fully responsive and works perfectly on Android, iPhone, tablets, and desktop browsers.
How do I receive payments?
Customers pay directly to your bank account. YAN-FLOWS sends your bank details via WhatsApp automatically with each order.
Is there a monthly subscription?
No. YAN-FLOWS uses a pay-per-order coin system. No monthly fees, no hidden charges.
